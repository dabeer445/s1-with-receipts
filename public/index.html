How the receipts work

  1. The S-1 is split into ~1,000 passages, each tagged with its section, heading and printed page.
  2. Your question pulls the most relevant passages (BM25, no embeddings).
  3. Claude may only answer with quotes copied from those passages, plus a short summary of what the quotes say.
  4. Code then checks every quote character-for-character against the filing. Anything that isn't there gets removed. If a quote is removed, the summary is hidden too. If nothing survives, you get "the filing doesn't say."
Open source: github.com/dabeer445/s1-with-receipts · Answers can still miss things the search didn't surface. Always check the section link.
